Organisation Overview
Name: N.L.Rikhotso Cancer Care Products Store

History of Our Cancer Care Products Store
The Cancer Care Products Store was established to provide high-quality products that support individuals undergoing cancer treatment and recovery. The store offers specialized products designed to enhance comfort, dignity, and quality of life. Collaborating closely with healthcare professionals and community organizations, the store ensures patients have access to trusted care solutions tailored to their needs.
Website Goals and Objectives
The primary objective is to create a professional, accessible, and informative website that showcases the store's products and services. The website will enable customers to browse products, place orders, access educational resources, and contact the store easily. Key performance indicators include online sales, enquiries, repeat customers, and customer satisfaction.
Current Website Analysis
Website Name
Dis-Chem Online Store
Strengths
•	Large product range: Offers a wide variety of health and cancer-support products in one place, making shopping convenient. 
•	Easy online shopping: The website has clear product categories, secure payment options, and home delivery across South Africa.
Weaknesses
•	Cancer products are difficult to find: Cancer care items are spread across different categories instead of having one dedicated cancer care section. 
•	Limited personalised guidance: There are few educational resources or personalised recommendations to help cancer patients choose suitable products.
Areas of improvement
•	Create a dedicated Cancer Care section: Organise products into categories such as Chemotherapy Care, Radiation Care, Nutrition, Wigs & Headwear, Post-Surgery Care, and Emotional Support, making products much easier to find.


Proposed Website Features and Functionality
The website will include the following pages: Home, About Us, Products, Shop, Educational Resources, Testimonials, and Contact. Key features will include secure online payments, live chat support, contact forms, and social media integration to enhance user experience and engagement.
Design and User Experience
The website will use soft pink, purple, and white colours to show hope and healing. It will feature clear navigation and readable texts. The responsive design ensures a smooth experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. High-quality images and a simple checkout process.
Technical Requirements
The website will be developed using HTML and a responsive framework. It will include a registered Domain.
Technical Requirements Summary
Compatibility: Desktop, tablet and mobile.
Security:  Backups and secure customer data management.
